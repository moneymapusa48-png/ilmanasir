WHAT TO EXPECT

What is skydiving, really?

Skydiving is a jump from an aircraft at altitude, followed by freefall before your parachute (the canopy) opens for a controlled ride down. At SkyRush, you climb to 13,000 feet, freefall for about 60 seconds near 120 mph, then glide back to the landing field under canopy for 5–7 minutes. No experience needed for tandem jumps — you're harnessed to your instructor the entire time.

EQUIPMENT

Built around backups

Every rig carries a main and reserve canopy, packed by a certified rigger.

M

Main & Reserve Canopy

Two independent parachutes on every rig. If the main doesn't open right, it's cut away and the reserve opens.

A

Automatic Activation Device

Monitors altitude and speed, firing the reserve automatically if needed — independent of instructor action.

A

Altimeter & Alarm

Every jumper wears a visual altimeter; instructors carry an audible alarm for deployment altitude.

H

Harness & Container

Tandem students are secured by a four-point harness, inspected before every lift.

H

Helmet & Jumpsuit

All jumpers wear a fitted helmet and jumpsuit; students also wear a radio for landing guidance.

P

Aircraft Maintenance

Jump aircraft follow an FAA-approved maintenance schedule with logged daily inspections.

SAFETY INFORMATION

Age, weight & medical requirements

Please review before booking.

18

Minimum Age: 18

Photo ID required at check-in. We do not offer jumps to minors, with or without parental consent.

W

Weight Limit: 220 lbs

Harness and canopy limits cap combined jumper weight — call ahead if near this limit.

☁

Weather Holds

Jumps delay for high winds, low cloud ceiling or storms. Safety calls are made the morning of your jump.

+

Medical Conditions

Recent surgery, pregnancy, heart conditions and some injuries can disqualify a jump. Tell us at booking.

✕

Alcohol & Substances

No jumper may fly under the influence. Instructors may ground any jumper at check-in.

✎

Waiver Required

All jumpers sign a liability waiver on-site. We email a copy once your booking is confirmed.

TRAINING & COURSES

The path from tandem to licensed jumper

Our Accelerated Freefall Program (AFF) is the fastest USPA-recognized route to a solo license.

1

Ground School

Full-day session covering exits, body position, canopy control and emergency procedures.

2

AFF Level 1–3

Freefall with two instructors holding on, practicing altitude checks and ripcord pulls.

3

AFF Level 4–6

One instructor flies alongside as you self-supervise exits and maneuvers.

4

AFF Level 7

Independent consolidation jumps, refining canopy control and landing accuracy.

5

A-License Checkride

Final supervised jump and written exam with a USPA Instructor Examiner.

6

Refresher & Static-Line

Half-day refreshers for returning jumpers; static-line courses available for groups.
